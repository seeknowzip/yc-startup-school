# Module 5 workbook

Use only when the user requests guided practice, a self-assessment, or a workbook. A module invocation alone does not require an interview.

Use the questions below as a bank, adapting them to the user's stage and already supplied answers. In a guided conversation, ask a manageable question and wait; use an available question interface or ordinary chat. Offering a few answer options can make a question easier to answer. After each answer, respond briefly and connect it to the relevant lecture before moving on. If the user wants the complete worksheet, provide it together. Skip irrelevant or answered questions, and respect requests to stop or change focus.

Read the relevant original material for the concepts used. Separate the user's answers, source-grounded observations, and your interpretation. Summarize supported strengths, gaps, and a useful next action when the requested exercise is complete. Do not invent a readiness score; if a score is requested, explain the rubric, evidence, and uncertainty.

Save only when a file is part of the user's request. Use their destination; if they ask to save without one, use a `yc-startup-school-notes/` folder in the current working directory with a descriptive dated filename. Preserve existing files and distinguish partial answers from a completed exercise. Otherwise finish in conversation.

## Question bank

1. Can you describe your product in one sentence that makes sense to a stranger — what it does, without jargon? (Kat Mañalac)
2. Have you launched at least once? What did you learn from that launch, and when will you launch again? (Kat Mañalac)
3. Who are the first 10 people you could personally reach out to today (not blast email — personally)? (Aaron Epstein; Gustaf Alströmer: your first customers should be your easiest)
4. What's the most manual, unscalable thing you could do right now to get 1 more paying user? (PG, "Do Things That Don't Scale"; Gustaf Alströmer)
5. Among your earliest users, which kind seem most enthusiastic — and where could you go to find more like them? (PG, "Do Things That Don't Scale")
6. Which communities are you already genuinely part of, and what would an authentic launch post there say? (Kat Mañalac)
7. What would make the experience of being your user insanely great this week, even with a buggy product, and how do users' reactions get back to you? (PG, "Do Things That Don't Scale")
8. If you haven't launched yet — what is the smallest thing you could ship and put in front of 3 real people this week? (PG, "Do Things That Don't Scale"; Kat Mañalac)
9. Working backward from a goal such as 10 demos, how many targeted outreach messages do you need, and which step of your funnel converts worst? (Aaron Epstein; Gustaf Alströmer)
